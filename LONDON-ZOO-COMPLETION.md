## 📖 Story Highlights

### Key Moments

1. **First Glimpse** 🌟
   - Arriving at the world's oldest zoo (almost 200 years old!)
   - Learning about ZSL (Zoological Society of London)
   - Over 750 species to discover

2. **Meeting the Giraffes** 🦒
   - Flo's amazement at their 18-foot height
   - Learning giraffes have only 7 neck bones (same as humans!)
   - Lexi's fascinating facts about nature's tallest animals

3. **Lions - The Sleepy Kings** 🦁
   - Watching the majestic lion family
   - Discovering lions sleep 20 hours a day
   - Flo teasing Lexi about her napping habits
   - Learning a lion's roar can be heard 5 miles away

4. **Penguin Beach** 🐧
   - Europe's largest penguin colony (70+ penguins!)
   - Flo making friends with a penguin through the glass
   - Learning penguins swim up to 22 mph underwater
   - The adorable waddle vs. graceful swimming contrast

5. **Reptile House** 🦎
   - Historic building from 1849 (over 175 years old!)
   - Giant Komodo dragons and colorful poison dart frogs
   - Learning how bright colors warn predators

6. **The Emotional Heart** ❤️
   - Sunset reflection on all their London adventures
   - Flo expressing how every adventure was better with Lexi
   - Lexi sharing how Flo's enthusiasm inspires her
   - Both realizing this is an ending AND a beginning
   - Promise to be "adventure buddies forever"
   - Looking forward to exploring the whole world together
