# Shopping Spree Files - Upload Guide

## Files You Need to Upload to GitHub

These 4 files are required for Adventure 15 (Shopping Spree) to work:

### Required Files:
1. **adventure-14-shopping-spree-part-1-hamleys.html**
2. **adventure-14-shopping-spree-part-2-disney-store.html**
3. **adventure-14-shopping-spree-part-3-harrods.html**
4. **adventure-14-shopping-spree-part-4-oxford-street.html**

---

## Step-by-Step Upload Process

### Step 1: Download Files from GenSpark
1. Go to your GenSpark project
2. Click "File Explorer" (left sidebar)
3. Find and download each of the 4 files listed above
4. Save them to your computer

### Step 2: Upload to GitHub
1. Go to https://github.com/floandlexi/floandlexi-website
2. Click "Add file" → "Upload files"
3. Drag all 4 downloaded files to the upload area
4. Add commit message: "Add shopping spree part files"
5. Click "Commit changes"

### Step 3: Wait for Deployment
1. After committing, GitHub will automatically deploy to Netlify
2. Wait 1-2 minutes for deployment to complete
3. Check https://floandlexi.com/adventure-15-shopping-spree.html
4. Click on any of the shopping spree parts - they should work now!

---

## Why This Happened

The shopping spree adventure is split into multiple files:
- **Main overview page**: adventure-15-shopping-spree.html (already uploaded)
- **Part 1**: adventure-14-shopping-spree-part-1-hamleys.html (missing)
- **Part 2**: adventure-14-shopping-spree-part-2-disney-store.html (missing)
- **Part 3**: adventure-14-shopping-spree-part-3-harrods.html (missing)
- **Part 4**: adventure-14-shopping-spree-part-4-oxford-street.html (missing)

The main page exists, but when you click on the parts, it can't find them because they weren't uploaded.

## Need Help?

If you're still seeing "page not found" after uploading:
1. Make sure the file names match exactly (including the "adventure-14" prefix)
2. Wait 2-3 minutes for Netlify to finish deploying
3. Clear your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R)
4. Try the links again
